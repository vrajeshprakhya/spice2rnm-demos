Stimulus: the pipeline's own equivalence check for this block, the input it drove into both the transistor circuit (ngspice) and the generated model (xezim).

How it was scored: rms of (model - circuit) over the whole run, divided by the circuit's swing (3.303 V); pass mark 15%.

The worst single point is at 15.85 ns: the circuit's output is at 1.74 V, partway through a swing, and the model's at 2.32 V (17.6% of the swing apart). Mid-swing is where an inverter's output moves fastest, so a small difference in when the model switches shows there as a large difference in volts, while the rms stays small. The "Worst point" button zooms there.

Every point the demo's equivalence check wrote is in this page (times to 1 fs, voltages to 0.1 µV); nothing was re-simulated to make it, and it loads nothing from the network. The same waveforms open in GTKWave from the .fst and .gtkw files. Built by harness/pll_wave.py in the spice2rnm demos.
