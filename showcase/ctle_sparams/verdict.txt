SDD21: the generated model against the transistors

how the model was measured:
  21 frequencies simulated, 1e+06 to 1e+10 Hz, drive 0.001 V about 0.9 V
  settling 1e-06 s before each measurement (5 time constants of the slowest fitted pole)
  8 periods integrated, 64 to 108004 samples per period (the step is capped at the model's own 9.259e-12 s, so a response faster than one step is still visible)
  the integer-femtosecond step moves each frequency by at most 320 ppm, and the reported frequency is the one driven
  2.44249e-05 s of model time simulated
  note: rounding the step to whole femtoseconds moved a frequency by 320 ppm; the reported frequency is the one driven, but a comparison against another sweep's grid is that far off

the conversion between a voltage response and an S-parameter:
  k = 2 sqrt(Zd_in/Zd_out) = 0.447213595
  checked against the .ac sweep of the same deck: worst |k*H_ac/SDD21 - 1| = 5.698e-09
  (that figure is the claim that both sides measure the same quantity; it is not an accuracy result)

compared over 21 frequencies, 21 of them in band (where the transistors' |SDD21| exceeds -40 dB)

over the WHOLE swept band, reported:
  worst |magnitude error|     0.1438 dB       at 10 GHz
  worst |phase error|        16.5148 degrees  at 10 GHz
  worst |group-delay error|  10.8669 ps       at 63.1 MHz

VERDICT: PASS

above 2.083 GHz the model cannot represent the response and these figures are REPORTED, NOT BARRED:
  worst |magnitude error|     0.1438 dB       at 10 GHz
  worst |phase error|        16.5148 degrees  at 10 GHz
  worst |group-delay error|   5.0909 ps       at 3.981 GHz
  a period up there is a handful of the model's own integration steps, so what a measurement finds depends on how finely it is driven.

the model under test integrates on a step whose ten-steps-per-period ceiling is 10.8 GHz, against the 2.083 GHz this band's edge was drawn at. THAT HAS MOVED: the band edge is a fixed number on purpose, so re-drawing it is a decision rather than a side effect

measured, not modelled:
  SDD11  input differential match          exactly zero at every frequency
  SDD22  output differential match         worst -46.116 dB at 10 GHz
  SDC21  common in, differential out       exactly zero at every frequency
  SCD21  differential in, common out       worst -331.133 dB at 125.9 MHz
  none of the four is represented in the generated model. An S-parameter port carries two travelling waves and an RNM net
  carries one real value per tick, so a reflection coefficient has nowhere to live in it. They are measured from the
  transistors and printed so that what the model does not cover is visible rather than absent.

passivity and reciprocity, derived from the measured S-matrix:
  largest singular value 0.743839168 at 10 GHz, smallest over the sweep 0.446443889
  that is at or below 1 + 1e-06, so no combination of incident waves gets power gain through these references
  and the passivity check APPLIES. The tolerance is for the simulator's own arithmetic rather than the physics:
  a network of R and C is passive exactly, so a few parts in a million either way is rounding, not gain.
  passivity, transistor side: 0.743839168 against 1 + 1e-06   PASS
    (the margin to 1 is 0.256160832, which is the network's own loss and not the tolerance)
  reciprocity, transistor side: worst |S(i,j) - S(j,i)| / |S| = 1.000e+00 at 1 MHz   REPORTED, NOT BARRED
    absolute worst 3.719e-01, and the ratio is the figure because what counts as small depends on how large the entries are.
    No bar, and the reason is physical: R, L and C make a reciprocal network and a transistor does not, so a
    limit here would fail every correct amplifier. A figure near zero on a deck of printed passives, though, is
    the claim that the measurement is of what the caller thinks -- which is why it is printed rather than dropped.
  passivity, SHIPPED MODEL: worst |SDD21| 0.744347914 at 3.981 GHz against 1 + 1e-06   PASS
  reciprocity is NOT measured on the shipped model at all, and the reason is structural rather than a shortcut. The model
  is measured by driving its input and reading its output; an RNM port carries one real value in one direction, so the
  model's SDD12 is a slot left at zero rather than a measurement. Comparing a measurement against a placeholder would
  report a failure that means nothing. The transistor side above is where reciprocity is observable, and it is observed
  there.
note: fitted from SDD21 of ctle_sp.s4p, k = 0.4472
