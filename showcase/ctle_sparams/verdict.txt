SDD21: the generated model against the transistors

how the model was measured:
  21 frequencies simulated, 1e+06 to 1e+10 Hz, drive 0.001 V about 0.9 V
  settling 1e-06 s before each measurement (5 time constants of the slowest fitted pole)
  8 periods integrated, 64 to 20830 samples per period (the step is capped at the model's own 4.801e-11 s, so a response faster than one step is still visible)
  the integer-femtosecond step moves each frequency by at most 320 ppm, and the reported frequency is the one driven
  2.44247e-05 s of model time simulated
  note: the model integrates on a 4.801e-11 s step, so its own ten-steps-per-period ceiling is 2.083e+09 Hz; 4 of 21 frequencies are above it (up to 1e+10 Hz) and what is measured there is the discretization, which is part of the shipped model but not part of the fit
  note: rounding the step to whole femtoseconds moved a frequency by 320 ppm; the reported frequency is the one driven, but a comparison against another sweep's grid is that far off

the conversion between a voltage response and an S-parameter:
  k = 2 sqrt(Zd_in/Zd_out) = 0.447213595
  checked against the .ac sweep of the same deck: worst |k*H_ac/SDD21 - 1| = 5.698e-09
  (that figure is the claim that both sides measure the same quantity; it is not an accuracy result)

compared over 21 frequencies, 21 of them in band (where the transistors' |SDD21| exceeds -40 dB)

over the WHOLE swept band, reported:

  worst |phase error|       101.2910 degrees  at 10 GHz
  worst |group-delay error|  37.3937 ps       at 10 GHz

VERDICT: PASS

above 2.083 GHz the model cannot represent the response and these figures are REPORTED, NOT BARRED:
  worst |magnitude error|     1.9084 dB       at 10 GHz
  worst |phase error|       101.2910 degrees  at 10 GHz
  worst |group-delay error|  37.3937 ps       at 10 GHz
  a period up there is a handful of the model's own integration steps, so what a measurement finds depends on how finely it is driven.

the model under test integrates on a step whose ten-steps-per-period ceiling is 2.083 GHz, against the 2.083 GHz this band's edge was drawn at

measured, not modelled:
  SDD11  input differential match          exactly zero at every frequency
  SDD22  output differential match         worst -46.116 dB at 10 GHz
  SDC21  common in, differential out       exactly zero at every frequency
  SCD21  differential in, common out       worst -331.133 dB at 125.9 MHz
  none of the four is represented in the generated model. An S-parameter port carries two travelling waves and an RNM net
  carries one real value per tick, so a reflection coefficient has nowhere to live in it. They are measured from the
  transistors and printed so that what the model does not cover is visible rather than absent.

no passivity check is applied here. This block is an amplifier: its largest singular value may exceed 1, and a passivity
check would fail it for working. The passivity check in this tool's tests runs against a passive RC fixture instead.
