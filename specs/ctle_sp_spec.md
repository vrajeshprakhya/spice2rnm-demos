# CTLE, differential: what the generated model has to reproduce

A continuous-time linear equaliser: a differential pair degenerated by a
parallel RS/CS, which boosts high frequency to undo a lossy channel.
Four ports: in+/in- at 50 ohm, out+/out- at 1 kohm, which is the load
the drains are.

## Function

The claim is about SDD21, the differential transmission. SDD11, SDD22,
SCD21 and SDC21 are measured from the transistors and reported, not
modelled: an S-parameter port carries two travelling waves and an RNM
net carries one real value per tick, so a reflection coefficient has
nowhere to live in it.

The small-signal response is a zero and a pole set by the degeneration:

| Parameter | Min | Typ | Max | Unit |
|-----------|-----|-----|-----|------|
| Zero, 1/(2 pi RS CS) | - | 99.47 | - | MHz |
| Pole, (1 + gm RS/2)/(2 pi RS CS) | - | 165.8 | - | MHz |

## S-parameter acceptance

| Band               | Up to     | Gain error | Phase error | Group delay error |
|--------------------|-----------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| equaliser          | 300 MHz   | 0.25 dB    | 1 deg       | 40 ps             |
| below_step_ceiling | 2.083 GHz | 0.25 dB    | 20 deg      | 40 ps             |

Each band is judged from 0 Hz up to its own edge. Above the last edge
the figures are reported and not judged.

Where the edges come from, and why they are written here as fixed
numbers rather than derived at run time:

* **equaliser, 300 MHz.** The zero is at 99 MHz and the pole at 166 MHz,
  so everything the block exists to do happens below this.
* **below_step_ceiling, 2.083 GHz.** This is where ten of the model's
  own 48.01 ps integration steps stop fitting in a period. Above it the
  model cannot represent the response at all -- at 10 GHz a period is
  two of its steps -- and what a measurement finds there depends on how
  finely it is driven, which is itself the evidence. A bar whose own
  band moves with the model it judges can absorb any regression by
  re-drawing itself, so this number is fixed and committed. A run
  reports separately when the model under test has a different ceiling;
  it never moves the edge.

What each limit was measured at when it was set, which is what makes it
a limit rather than a wish:

| Band | Metric | Limit | Measured | Margin |
|------|--------|-------|----------|--------|
| equaliser | gain | 0.25 dB | 0.0788 dB at 251 MHz | 3.2x |
| equaliser | phase | 1 deg | 0.272 deg at 100 MHz | 3.7x |
| equaliser | group delay | 40 ps | 15.6 ps at 1.58 MHz | 2.6x |
| below_step_ceiling | gain | 0.25 dB | 0.0788 dB at 251 MHz | 3.2x |
| below_step_ceiling | phase | 20 deg | 10.96 deg at 1.58 GHz | 1.8x |
| below_step_ceiling | group delay | 40 ps | 24.3 ps at 1.58 GHz | 1.6x |

The margins are in line with the 3.85x the tool's own time-domain CTLE
golden carries (0.038997 measured against a 0.15 threshold).

## Passivity

Nothing is declared here, because nothing needs to be: the largest
singular value of the measured S-matrix is what says whether a passivity
check applies.

On this block it comes back at **0.744 at 10 GHz**, so the check is
applied and passes with 0.256 of margin. That is worth stating plainly,
because the deck is an amplifier and the figure reads like the opposite:
SDD21 is a WAVE ratio, and the output pair's 2 kohm differential
reference against the input pair's 100 ohm turns +3.48 dB of
differential voltage gain into -3.51 dB of wave gain. The block delivers
voltage gain into a high impedance; it does not deliver power gain into
these references, and a singular value is about power.

Reciprocity is reported and never barred. This block's worst relative
asymmetry is 1.0 -- S13 is exactly zero against an S31 of -13.025 dB --
which is what the transistors in it look like, and a bar there would
fail a correct amplifier. On a deck of printed passives the same figure
near zero is the claim that the measurement is of what the reader
thinks, which is why it is printed rather than dropped.
